Ada Lovelace

Analytical engines, mostly.

About

Mathematician and writer, chiefly known for her work on Charles Babbage's proposed Analytical Engine. She translated Menabrea's 1843 sketch of the engine and appended seven notes of her own, signed A.A.L., which run to roughly three times the length of the original paper.
