title: Music school timetable and termly billing
---
we run a small music school out of a church hall. about 90 kids, 7 teachers,
piano guitar drums and singing.

the whole thing runs on a shared spreadsheet and my memory, and it falls over
every single term. what I need is something that does the timetable and the money
together, because at the moment they're two separate things and the gap between
them is exactly where it goes wrong.

lessons are weekly, same slot every week through the term, 30 or 45 minutes.
mostly one to one but the guitar group has four kids in it at once. each teacher
has their own availability, and two of them share the small room so you can't put
them both in there at the same time.

then real life happens. kids are ill, teachers are ill, half term exists, and
someone always wants to move to a different slot from week six. if we cancel a
lesson we owe them a credit or a make up, if they cancel on the morning they
don't get one. that rule is the thing I end up arguing about with parents every
term so it has to be right and I have to be able to show them why.

at the end of term I invoice each family for what they actually had, and pay each
teacher for what they actually taught, and those two numbers have to reconcile
against each other. some families have two or three kids with us, and there are a
couple on the hardship rate who pay less.

everything's in the spreadsheet now and I'm not typing 90 kids in again.

I need to be able to trust the numbers on this one. last term I overcharged a
family for three lessons they never had and it was mortifying.
