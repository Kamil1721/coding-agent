title: Job tracker with logins for a small plumbing firm
---
right, we need to get off the spreadsheet.

there's four of us and we all update the same google sheet with our jobs and it's
a mess. people overwrite each other, and last month someone deleted a whole
column and we lost about a week of work. I've had enough of it.

what I want is a little web thing where each of us logs in and sees the jobs. a
job is a customer, an address, what needs doing, when it's booked in, which of
the lads is on it, and whether it's quoted, booked, done or invoiced. we need to
add jobs, change them when the customer moves it (happens constantly), and mark
them off when they're finished.

obviously nobody outside the four of us should be able to see any of it, there's
customer addresses and phone numbers in there.

it would also be really useful to know who changed what and when, because of the
column thing. and customers ring up asking "when are you coming" so being able to
search by name or postcode would save us a lot of hassle. plus seeing what's on
this week at a glance, that's the main thing I look at.

design doesn't matter, it just has to work on my phone in the van and on the
laptop in the office.
