ok this is the one I keep going on about. build me a golf app.

I play twice a week, usually round three or four courses near me, and I want to
stop keeping score on the back of the card and then losing it in the car. so on
my phone I start a round, pick the course, and tap the score in as I go. it
should already know the holes and the pars for that course so I'm not typing all
that in every time - can it pull the course info in from somewhere? there's a
golf course database thing, my mate's app does it.

then the bit I actually care about: over time, tell me something useful. how I'm
doing hole by hole, which holes I always blow up on, whether my handicap is
actually moving or I'm kidding myself. and I want to be able to look back at last
summer and compare.

my brother in law would use it too and we're competitive, so seeing each other's
rounds would be good. we play different courses most of the time though.

it has to keep working when there's no signal. half of my home course has nothing
at all and I am not losing a round because of it - that's the thing that annoys
me most about the app I use now.

and please make sure it actually works properly. the last thing I paid someone to
build looked finished and then quietly lost two of my rounds and I only noticed
weeks later.
