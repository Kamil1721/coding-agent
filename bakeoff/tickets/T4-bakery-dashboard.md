title: Wholesale bakery production dashboard
---
we bake for about 25 cafes and delis around the city and right now the order book
is a whatsapp group and my head baker's notebook. it works until someone's off.

what I want is a screen we can stick on the wall in the bakery that shows what
has to be made tonight. so all of tomorrow's orders added up by product - 40
sourdough, 60 seeded, 30 of the rye - and then split out by which cafe it goes to
so the drivers know what to load.

the standing orders are the big thing. most places have the same order every
week, mondays wednesdays fridays, twenty loaves, that sort of arrangement. then
they ring up and change it for a bank holiday, or add 10 extra because they've
got an event on, or skip a week in january when they're dead. so it has to handle
the regular stuff and the one-offs sitting on top of it.

I'd also like to look back over the last few months and see whether we're baking
too much. we chuck a fair bit away on tuesdays and I want to know if that's a real
pattern or just me moaning.

only the four of us in the bakery would ever use it, nobody needs to get at it
from home. and the wall screen shouldn't need someone to keep clicking it, it
just sits there.
